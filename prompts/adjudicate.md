Two independent readings of the same vendor document disagree about some RFx lines, or only one of them found an offer for a line. For each disputed line you get the RFx line and the readings; the whole vendor document follows. Decide what the document actually says for each line.

For each disputed line, in the order given, give one decision:
- A or B: that reading is what the document says for this line.
- THIRD: neither reading is right, and the document says something else for this line. Give it exactly as written, with its snippet and locator.
- NO_OFFER: the document does not price this line; a reading that found an offer mistook another line's price, an alternate, a superseded value or a history quote for an offer on this line.
- UNRESOLVED: the document is ambiguous or unreadable for this line, or it could fairly be read more than one way. Say exactly what is ambiguous.

Decide from the document, not from what seems likely: do not prefer the cheaper price, the more plausible price, or the reading that agrees with other vendors. When a reading rests on a judgment (which line a phrase like "3-ply" covers, a unit carried over from the same sentence), choose it only if the document supports that judgment clearly; otherwise answer UNRESOLVED. Keep reasoning to one or two sentences that cite the words that decide it.

For A, B and NO_OFFER, leave the value, snippet and locator fields "". For THIRD, fill them following the conventions below. For UNRESOLVED, leave them "".
